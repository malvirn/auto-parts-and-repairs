Accounting

Every module below shares one general ledger — nothing here stands alone.

Ledger
Chart of accounts, journal entries, and the Trial Balance everything else posts against.
Payables
What you owe each supplier, due dates, and aging on unpaid bills.
Cash Book
Live cash and bank movements, plus the petty cash float.
Commissions
Technician and salesperson commission, folded into Payroll automatically.
Landed Cost
Freight, duty, and insurance allocated across a shipment's parts by value.
Fixed Assets
Equipment register with straight-line depreciation and disposal handling.
Statements
Profit & Loss for a period, and a Balance Sheet as of any date.
