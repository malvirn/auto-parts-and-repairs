Business details

Shown on job cards, receipts and quotations.

Customer status emails require RESEND_API_KEY and STATUS_FROM_EMAIL in Supabase Edge Function secrets. Use a sender address verified with your email provider.

The current document workflow applies one business VAT rate across taxable items. Zero-rated and exempt item-level treatment, plus input VAT recovery on purchases, still require a full tax-ledger upgrade.

FDMS is not connected.

ZIMRA states that all taxpayers are required to fiscalise. VAT-registered operators must use an approved fiscal device or virtual fiscal device interfaced with FDMS. Centric is not currently connected to a fiscal device or FDMS, so its invoices and receipts are management documents, not ZIMRA fiscal tax invoices or receipts. Do not use Centric as your only fiscalisation solution until a compliant device or integration is in place. Read ZIMRA fiscalisation requirements.

Labour charges

Charge per hour for each kind of vehicle. A new job card picks the charge from the vehicle's make; staff can still change it on the job.

Makes are comma-separated (for example: Toyota, Honda). Leave makes empty for a charge that is only picked by hand. One charge is the default for makes that don't match.
