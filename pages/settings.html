Labour charges

Charge per hour for each kind of vehicle. A new job card picks the charge from the vehicle's make; staff can still change it on the job.

Makes are comma-separated (for example: Toyota, Honda). Leave makes empty for a charge that is only picked by hand. One charge is the default for makes that don't match.
